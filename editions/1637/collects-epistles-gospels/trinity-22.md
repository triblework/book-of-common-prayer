# The xxij. Sunday after Trinity

## The Collect

LORD we beseech thee to keep thy houshold the Church in continuall godlinesse: that through thy protection it may be free from all adversities, and devoutly given to serve thee in good works, to the glory of thy name, through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

## The Epistle

Philippians 1:3

## The Gospel

Matthew 18:21
