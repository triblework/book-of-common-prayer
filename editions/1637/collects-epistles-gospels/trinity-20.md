# The xx. Sunday after Trinity

## The Collect

ALmighty and merciful God, of thy bountiful goodness keep us from all things that may hurt us: that we being ready both in body and soul, may with free hearts accomplish those things that thou wouldest have done, through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

## The Epistle

Ephesians 5:15

## The Gospel

Matthew 22:1
