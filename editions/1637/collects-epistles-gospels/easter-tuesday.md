# Tuesday in Easter week

## The Collect

ALmighty Father, which hast given thine only Son to dye for our sins, and to rise again for our justification: grant us so to put away the leaven of malice and wickednesse, that we may allway serve thee in purenesse of living and truth, through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.
