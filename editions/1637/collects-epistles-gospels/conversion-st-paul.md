# The Conversion of S. Paul

## The Collect

GOD, which hast taught all the world, through the preaching of thy blessed Apostle Saint Paul: grant we beseech thee, that we which have his wonderfull conversion in remembrance, may follow and fulfill the holy doctrine that he taught, through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

Jan. 25

## The Epistle

Acts 9:1

## The Gospel

Matthew 19:27
