# Saint John Baptist

## The Collect

ALmighty God, by whose providence thy servant Saint John Baptist was wonderfully born, and sent to prepare the way of thy Sonne our Saviour, by preaching of penance: make us so to follow his doctrine and holy life, that wee may truely repent according to his preaching; and after his example constantly speak the truth, boldly rebuke vice, and paciently suffer for the truths sake, through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

June 25
