# The Purification of Saint

## The Collect

Mary the Virgin.

ALmightie and everlasting God, we humbly beseech thy Majesty, that as thy onely begotten Son was this day presented in the Temple in the substance of our flesh: so grant that we may bee presented unto thee with pure and cleare minds, by Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.
