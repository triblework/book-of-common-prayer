# Saint Thomas the Apostle

## The Collect

ALmighty and everliving God, which for the more confirmation of the Faith, didst suffer thy holy Apostle Saint Thomas to be doubtfull in thy Sonnes Resurrection: grant us so perfectly, and without all doubt to beleeve in thy Sonne Jesus Christ, that our Faith in thy sight never bee reproved. Hear us, O Lord, through the same Jesus Christ, to whom with thee and the holy Ghost bee all honour, &c.

Dec. 21
