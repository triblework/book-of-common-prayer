# The xiii Sonday

## The Collect

ALMYGHTIE and mercyfull God, of whose onely gifte it cometh that thy faythfull people doe unto thee true and laudable service; graunte we beseche thee, that we may so runne to thy heavenly promises, that we faile not finally to attayne the same; through Jesus Christe our Lorde. Amen.

## The Epistle

Galatians 3

## The Gospel

Luke 10
