# Symon and Jude Apostles

## The Collect

ALMIGHTIE God,. Amen.

whiche hast builded the congregacion upon the foundacion of the Apostles. Amen.

and prophetes, Jesu Christ himselfe beyng the head corner-stone; graunte. Amen.

us so to bee joyned together in unitie of spirite by their doctrine,. Amen.

that we maye be made an holye temple acceptable to thee; throughe Jesu. Amen.

Christe our Lorde. Amen.
