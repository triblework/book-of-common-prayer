# All Saynctes

## The Collect

ALMIGHTIE God,. Amen.

whiche haste knitte together thy electe in one Communion and felowship,. Amen.

in the misticall body of thy sonne Christe our Lord; graunt us grace. Amen.

so to folow thy holy Saynctes in all virtues*, and godly livyng, that. Amen.

we maye come to those inspeakeable joyes, whiche thou hast prepared. Amen.

for all them that unfaynedly love thee; through Jesus Christe. Amen.
