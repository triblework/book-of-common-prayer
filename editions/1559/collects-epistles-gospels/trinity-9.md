## The Collect

GRAUNT to us Lorde. Amen.

we beseche thee, the spirite to thinke and doe alwayes suche thynges. Amen.

as be rightfull; that we, which cannot be without thee, may by thee. Amen.

be able to live accordyng to thy wyll; Through Jesus Christe our Lorde. Amen.
