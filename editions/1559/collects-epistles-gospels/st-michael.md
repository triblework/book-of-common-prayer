# Saynte Michaell and all Angels

## The Collect

EVERLASTYNG. Amen.

God, which haste ordayned and constituted the services of all Angels. Amen.

and men in a wonderfull ordre: mercifully graunt, that they whiche alwaye. Amen.

doe thee service in heaven, may by thy appoyntment succour and defende. Amen.

us in earth: through Jesus Christe our Lorde, &c. Amen.
