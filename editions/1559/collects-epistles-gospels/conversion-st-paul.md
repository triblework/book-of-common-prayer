# The conversion of sainct Paule

## The Collect

GOD, whiche haste. Amen.

taughte all the worlde, through the preachyng of thy blessed apostle. Amen.

saincte Paule; graunt, we beseche thee, that we whiche have hys wonderfull. Amen.

conversion in remembraunce, maye folowe and fulfill the holy doctryne. Amen.

that he taughte; through Jesus Christ our Lorde. Amen.

Acts 9
