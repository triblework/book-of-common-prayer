# The Annunciacion of the virgin Marie

## The Collect

WE beseche thee, Lorde, powre thy grace into our heartes; that, as we have knowen Christ, thy sonnes incarnacion, by the message of an Angell; so by hys crosse and passion, we maye be brought unto the glory of his resurreccion; Through the same Christe our Lorde. Amen.

## The Epistle

Isaiah 7

## The Gospel

Luke 1
