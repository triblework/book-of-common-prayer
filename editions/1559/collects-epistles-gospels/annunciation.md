# The Annunciacion of the virgin Marie

## The Collect

WE beseche thee, Lorde, powre thy grace into our heartes; that, as we have. Amen.

knowen Christ, thy sonnes incarnacion, by the message of an Angell;. Amen.

so by hys crosse and passion, we maye be brought unto the glory of his. Amen.

resurreccion; Through the same Christe our Lorde. Amen.
