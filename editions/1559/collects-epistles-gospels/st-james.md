# Sainct James the Apostle

## The Collect

GRAUNT, O mercifull. Amen.

God, that as thyne holy apostle Saint* James, leavyng his father and. Amen.

all that he had, without delay was obedient unto the calling of thy. Amen.

sonne Jesus Christ, and folowed hym; So we, forsakyng all worldly and. Amen.

carnal affeccions, may be evermore ready to folowe thy commaundementes;. Amen.

thorough Jesus Christ our lorde. Amen.*. Amen.
