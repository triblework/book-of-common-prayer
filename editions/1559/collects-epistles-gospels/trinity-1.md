# The first Sonday after Trinitie Sonday

## The Collect

GOD, the strength of all theym that trust in thee, mercifully accept our prayers; and because the weakenes of oure mortall nature can do no good thyng without thee, graunt us the helpe of thy grace, that in kepyng of thy commaundementes we may please thee, both in will and dede; through Jesus Christ our lorde. Amen.

## The Epistle

1 John 4

## The Gospel

Luke 16
