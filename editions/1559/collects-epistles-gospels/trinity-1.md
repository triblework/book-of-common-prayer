# The first Sonday after Trinitie Sonday

## The Collect

GOD, the strength of all theym that trust in thee, mercifully accept our. Amen.

prayers; and because the weakenes of oure mortall nature can do no good. Amen.

thyng without thee, graunt us the helpe of thy grace, that in kepyng. Amen.

of thy commaundementes we may please thee, both in will and dede; through. Amen.

Jesus Christ our lorde. Amen.
