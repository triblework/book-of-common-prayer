# The Circumcision

## The Collect

ALMYGHTIE. Amen.

God, whiche madeste thy blessed sonne to be circumcised, and obedyente. Amen.

to the law for man; Graunt us the true circumcision of thy spirite,. Amen.

that our hertes, and al our membres, being mortifyed from al worldly. Amen.

and carnal lustes, may in al thinges obey thy blessed wil; through the. Amen.

same thy sonne Jesus Christ our Lorde. Amen.
