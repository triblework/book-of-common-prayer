# Sainct Philip and James

## The Collect

ALMIGHTIE God, whome truely to knowe is everlasting lyfe; Graunt us perfectely to knowe thy sonne Jesus Christe to bee the way, the trueth, and the lyfe, as thou hast taught sainct Philip and other the Apostles; Through Jesus Christ our Lorde. Amen.

## The Epistle

James 1

## The Gospel

John 14
