# Sainct James the Apostle

## The Collect

GRAUNT, O mercifull God, that as thyne holy apostle Saint* James, leavyng his father and all that he had, without delay was obedient unto the calling of thy sonne Jesus Christ, and folowed hym; So we, forsakyng all worldly and carnal affeccions, may be evermore ready to folowe thy commaundementes; thorough Jesus Christ our lorde. Amen.*

## The Epistle

Acts 11 and 12

## The Gospel

Matthew 20
