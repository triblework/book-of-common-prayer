# In the tyme of any common plague or syckenesse.

O ALMIGHTYE God, whiche in thy wrathe, in the tyme of king David didst slea [slay] with the plague of pestilence, thre score and ten thousande, and yet remebringe thy mercy, diddest save the rest: have pitie upon us miserable synners, that nowe are vysited with great sicknesse, and mortalitie, that like as thou diddest then commaunde thyne angel to cease from punishing; So it may now please thee to withdrawe from us this plague, and grevous syckenesse, throughe Jesus Christe oure Lorde. Amen.
