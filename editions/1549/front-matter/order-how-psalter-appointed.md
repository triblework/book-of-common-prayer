# The Order how the Psalter is appointed to be read

THE Psalter shalbe red through once every Moneth, and because that some Moneths, be longer then some other be; it is thought good, to make them even by this meanes.

To every Moneth, as concernyng this purpose, shall be appointed just xxx dayes.

And because January and Marche hath one daye, above the sayd noumbre, and February, whiche is placed betwene them bothe, hath onely xxviii daies, February shall borowe of either of the Moneths of January and Marche one daie, and so the Psalter which shalbe red in February, muste bee begon the last daie of January, and ended the first daie of Marche.

And whereas Maie, July, August, October and December, have xxxi dayes apeece, it is ordered that the same Psalmes shall be redde the laste daye of the sayed Monethes, whiche were red the daie before: so that the Psalter maye bee begon agayne the firste daye of the nexte Moneths ensuyng.

Now to knowe what Psalmes shalbe red every daye, loke in the Kalendar the nombre that is appoynted for the Psalmes, and then finde the same nombre in this Table, and upon that nombre shall you se, what Psalmes shalbe sayd at Matyns, and Evensong.

And where the cxix. Psalme is devided into xxii porcions, and is over long to be red at one tyme: it is so ordered, that at one tyme shall not be red above iiii. or v. of the saied porcions, as you shall perceive to be noted in this Table.

And here is also to bee noted, that in this Table, and in all other partes of ye service, where any Psalmes are appoincted, the nombre is expressed after the greate English Bible, whiche from the ix. Psalme unto the cxlviii. Psalme (folowyng the division of the Ebrues [Hebrew]) doth vary in nombres from the common Latyn translacion.
