OMOST gracious Father, we fly unto thee for mercyin behalf of this thy servant, here lying under the
sudden visitation of thine hand. If it be thy will, pre-
serve his life, that there may be place for repenta nce;
but if thou hast otherwise appointed, let thy mercy
supply to him the want of the usual opportunity for
the trimming of his lamp.  Stir up in him such sorrow
for sin, and such fervent love to thee, as may in a
short time do the work of many days: That among the
praises which thy saints and holy angels shall sing to
the honour of thy mercy through eternal ages, it may
be to thy unspeakable glory, that thou hast redeemed
the soul of this thy servant from eternal death, and
made him partaker of the everlasting life, which is
through Jesus Christ our Lord.  Amen.
