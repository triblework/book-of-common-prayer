# The vj. Sunday after Trinity

## The Collect

GOD, which hast prepared to them that love thee, such good things as passe all mans understanding: powre into our hearts such love towards thee, that we loving thee in all things, may obtaine thy promises, which exceed all that wee can desire, through Jesus Christ our Lord.
Amen.

## The Epistle

Romans 6:3

## The Gospel

Matthew 5:20
