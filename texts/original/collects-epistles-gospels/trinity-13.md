# The xiij. Sunday after Trinity

## The Collect

ALmighty and mercifull God, of whose onely gift it cometh, that thy faithfull people do unto thee true and laudable service: Grant we beseech thee, that we may so runne to thy heavenly promises, that we fail not finally to attain the same, through Jesus Christ our Lord.
Amen.

## The Epistle

Galatians 3:16

## The Gospel

Luke 10:23
