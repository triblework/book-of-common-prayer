## The Collect

GRAUNTE, we beseche.
Amen.

thee, almyghtye God, that we, whiche for oure evill dedes are worthely.
Amen.

punyshed, by the comforte of thy grace may mercyfully be releved; through.
Amen.

our Lorde Jesus Christe.
Amen.
