## The Collect

ALMYGHTYE and

everlastyng God, mercifullye looke upon oure infirmities, and in al

our daungiers and necessities, stretche foorth thy ryghte hande to helpe

and defende us; through Christ our Lorde.

## The Epistle

Romans 12

## The Gospel

Matthew 8
