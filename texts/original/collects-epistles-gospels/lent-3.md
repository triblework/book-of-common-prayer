# The third Sunday in Lent

## The Collect

WE beseech thee, almighty God, look upon the hearty desires of thy humble servants, and stretch forth the right hand of thy Majestie, to bee oure defence against all our enemies; through Jesus Christe oure Lord.
Amen.
