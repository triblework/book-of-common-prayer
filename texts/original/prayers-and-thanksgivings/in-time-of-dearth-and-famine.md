# In the tyme of dearth and famyne.

O GOD heavenly Father, whose gyft it is that the rayne dothe fall, the earth is frutefull, beastes encrease, and fyshes do multiply: Behold, we beseche thee, the afflictions of thy people, and graunte that the scarcitye and dearthe (which we do now moste justly suffer for our iniquitye) maye through thy goodnes be mercifully tourned into cheapnesse and plenty, for the love of Jesu Christ our Lorde, to whome with thee and the holye Ghoste be Prayse for ever.
Amen.
