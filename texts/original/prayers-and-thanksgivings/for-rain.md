# For raine, if the time require.

O God, heavenly Father, whose gift is that the raine doth fall, and the earth is fruitfull, beasts increase, and fishes do multiply: Send us we beseech thee in this our necessitie, such moderate raine and showres, that we may receive the fruits of the earth to our comfort, and to thy honour; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
Amen.
