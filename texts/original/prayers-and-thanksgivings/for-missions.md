# For Missions.

O GOD, who hast made of one blood all nations of men for to dwell on the face of the whole earth, and didst send thy blessed Son to preach peace to them that are far off and to them that are nigh; Grant that all men everywhere may seek after thee and find thee.
Bring the nations into thy fold, and add the heathen to thine inheritance.
And we pray thee shortly to accomplish the number of thine elect, and to hasten thv kingdom; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
Amen.
