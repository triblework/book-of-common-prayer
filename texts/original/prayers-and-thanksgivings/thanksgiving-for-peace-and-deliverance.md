# A thankesgiving for peace and victory.

O Almighty GOD, which art a strong Tower of defence unto thy servants against the face of their enemies : We yield thee praise and thankesgiving for our deliverance from those great and apparent dangers wherewith we were compassed : Wee acknowledge it thy goodnesse that wee were not delivered over as a prey unto them; beseeching thee still to continue such thy mercies towards us, that all the world may know that thou art our Saviour and mighty deliverer; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
Amen.
