# A thankesgiving for faire Weather.

O LORD GOD, who hast justly humbled us by thy punishment of immoderate raine and waters, and in thy mercy hast relieved and comforted our soules by this seasonable and blessed change of weather : We praise and glorify thy holy Name for this thy mercie, and will always declare thy loving kindnesse from generation to generation; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
Amen.
