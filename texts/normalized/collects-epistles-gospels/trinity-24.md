# The xxiiii Sondaye

## The Collect

LORD we beseche

thee, assoyle thy people from their

offences, that through thy bountiful goodnes we maye bee delyvered from

the handes of all those sins, whiche by our frayltye we have committed

: Graunt this, &c.

Colossians 1

## The Gospel

Matthew 9
