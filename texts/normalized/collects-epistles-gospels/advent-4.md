# The fourth Sunday in Advent

## The Collect

LORD raise up (we pray thee) thy power, and come among us, and with great might succour us, that whereas through our sins and wickednesse, we be sore let and hindred, thy bountiful grace and mercy (through the satisfaction of thy Sonne our Lord) may spedily deliver us: to whom with thee and the holy Ghost be honour and glory world without end.
Amen.

## The Epistle

Philippians 4:4

## The Gospel

John 1:19
