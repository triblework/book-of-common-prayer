# The fifth Sunday after the Epiphany

## The Collect

LORD, we beseech thee to keep thy Church and houshold continually in thy true religion, that they which do lean only upon hope of thy heavenly grace may evermore bee defended by thy mighty power; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
Amen.

## The Epistle

Colossians 3:12

## The Gospel

> The sixth Sunday (if there be so many) shall have the same Collect, Epistle, and Gospel, that was upon the fifth Sunday.

Matthew 13:24
