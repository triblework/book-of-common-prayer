# The xxiij. Sunday after Trinity

## The Collect

GOD, our refuge and strength, which art the author of all godlinesse, be ready to heare the devout prayers of thy Church: and grant that those things which wee ask faithfully, wee may obtain effectually, through Jesu Christ our Lord.
Amen.

## The Epistle

Philippians 3:17

## The Gospel

Matthew 22:15
