# The ix Sonday

## The Collect

GRAUNT to us Lorde

we beseche thee, the spirit to thinke and doe alwayes suche thynges

as be rightful; that we, which cannot be without thee, may by thee

be able to live accordyng to thy wyll; Through Jesus Christe our Lorde.
