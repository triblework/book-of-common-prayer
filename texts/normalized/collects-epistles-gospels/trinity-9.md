# The ix. Sunday after Trinity

## The Collect

GRant us Lord, wee beseech thee, the Spirit to think and do allwayes such things as be rightful; that we, which cannot bee without thee, may by thee be able to live according to thy will, through Jesus Christ our Lord.
Amen.

## The Epistle

1 Corinthians 10:1

## The Gospel

Luke 16:1
