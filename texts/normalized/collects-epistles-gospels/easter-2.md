# The ii. Sunday after Easter

## The Collect

ALmighty God, which hast given thine only Son to be unto us both a sacrifice for sin, and also an example of godly life, give us the grace that we may allwayes most thankfully receive that his inestimable benefit, and also daily endeavour our selves to follow the blessed steps of his moste holy life through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
Amen.

## The Epistle

1 Peter 2:19

## The Gospel

John 10:11
