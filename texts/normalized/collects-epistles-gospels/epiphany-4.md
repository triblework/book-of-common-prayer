# The iiij. Sunday after the Epiphany

## The Collect

GOD which knowest us to bee set in the midst of so many and great dangers, that for mans frailnesse we cannot allwayes stand uprightly: grant to us the health of body and soul, that all those things which wee suffer for sin, by thy help wee may well passe and overcome; through Christ our Lord.
Amen.

## The Epistle

Romans 13:1

## The Gospel

Matthew 8:23
