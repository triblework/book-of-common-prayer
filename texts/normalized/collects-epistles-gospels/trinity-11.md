# The xj. Sunday after Trinity

## The Collect

GOD, which declarest thy Almighty power, most chiefly in shewing mercy and pity, give unto us abundantly thy grace, that wee, running to thy promises, may be made partakers of thy heavenly treasure, through Jesus Christ our Lord.
Amen.

## The Epistle

1 Corinthians 15:1

## The Gospel

Luke 18:9
