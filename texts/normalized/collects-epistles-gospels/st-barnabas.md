# Saint Barnabe Apostle

## The Collect

LORD Almighty, whiche hast indued thy holy Apostle Saint Barnabas with singular gifts of thy holy Ghost: let us not be destitute of thy manifold gifts, nor yet of grace to use them alway to thy honour and glory, through Jesus Christ our Lord.
Amen.

Saint Barnabus

June 11
