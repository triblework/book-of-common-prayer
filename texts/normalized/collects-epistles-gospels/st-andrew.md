# Saint Andrews day

## The Collect

ALmighty God, which didst give such grace unto thy holy Apostle Saint Andrew, that he readily obeyed the calling of thy Sonne Jesus Christ, and followed him without delay: grant unto us all, that wee being called by thy holy word, may forthwith give over our selves obediently to fulfill thy holy Commandements, through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
Amen.

Nov. 30

## The Epistle

Romans 10:9

## The Gospel

Matthew 4:18
