# Sainct Mary Magdalene

## The Introit

Lauda, anima mea.
Psalm 146

## The Collect

MERCYFUL father,

geve us grace, that we never presume to sin thorough the example of

any creature; but if it shall chaunce us at any tyme to offende thy

divine majesty; that then we may truely repent, and lament the same,

after the example of Mary Magdalene, and by lyvely faith obtaine remission

of all our sins; through the only merites of thy sonne our saviour

Christ.
