# The Innocentes

## The Collect

ALMIGHTY God,.
Amen.

whose prayse this daye the yong innocentes thy witnesses hath confessed.
Amen.

and showed forth, not in speakyng but in dying; Mortifye and kyll all.
Amen.

vyces in us, that in oure conuersacion.
Amen.

oure lyfe maye expresse thy faith, whiche with oure tongues we doe confesse;.
Amen.

through Jesus Christe oure Lord.
Amen.
