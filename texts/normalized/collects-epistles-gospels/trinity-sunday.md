# Trinity Sunday

## The Collect

ALmighty and everlasting God, whiche hast given unto us thy servants grace, by the confession of a true faith, to acknowlege the glory of the eternall Trinity, and in the power of the divine Majestie to worship the Unitie: Wee beseech thee that through the stedfastnesse of this faith, we may evermore be defended from all adversity, which livest and reignest, one God, world without end.
Amen.
