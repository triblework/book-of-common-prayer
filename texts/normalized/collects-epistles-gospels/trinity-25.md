# The xxv Sondaye

## The Collect

STIERE

up we beseche thee, O Lord, the wylles of thy faythful people, that

they, plenteously bringing furth the fruite of good workes; may of thee,

be plenteously rewarded; through Jesus Christe our Lorde.
Amen.
