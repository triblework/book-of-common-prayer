# The Ascension day

## The Collect

GRant we beseech thee Almighty God, that like as we do beleeve thy only begotten Sonne our Lord to have ascended into the heavens: So we may also in heart and mind thither ascend, and with him continually dwell, who liveth and reigneth with thee and the holy Ghost, one God, world without end.

## The Epistle

Acts 1:1

## The Gospel

Mark 16:14
