# S. John Evangelists day

## The Collect

MErciful LORD, we beseech thee to cast thy bright beams of light upon thy Church, that it being lightned by the doctrine of thy blessed Apostle and Evangelist Saint John, may attain to thy everlasting gifts, through Jesus Christe our Lord.
Amen.

## The Epistle

1 John 1:1

## The Gospel

John 21:19
