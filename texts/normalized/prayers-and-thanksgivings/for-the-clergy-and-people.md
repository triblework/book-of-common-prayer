# A Prayer for the holy Clergie

ALmighty and everlasting GOD, who only workest great and marvellous things, send down upon our Bishops, Presbyters and Curates, and all Congregations committed to their charge, the healthful Spirit of thy grace; and that they may truly please thee, poure upon them the continuall dew of thy blessing : Grant this, O Lord, for the honour of our Advocate and Mediatour, Jesus Christ.
Amen.
