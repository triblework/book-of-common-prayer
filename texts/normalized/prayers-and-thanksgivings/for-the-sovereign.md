# Prayer for the Queenes Majesty.

<!-- VERIFY: 'Quene Elizabeth' — the 1559 page's apparatus says this prayer was replaced by a prayer for the King in 1604 and gives the style 'Sovereign Lord King James', but attests neither the pronouns nor the spellings the 1604 book printed. The 1559 wording is retained and the gap recorded; resolve from a 1604 facsimile. -->

O LORD our hevenly father, high and mighty king of kynges, Lorde of lordes, the only ruler of princes, which doest from thy throne beholde all the dwellers upon earth, most hartely we beseche the with thy favoure to beholde our mooste gracious soveraigne Lady Quene Elizabeth*, and so replenyshe her with the grace of thy holy spirit, that she may alway incline to thy wil, and walcke in thy waye: Indue her plentifully wyth heavenly gifts: Graunt her in health and wealthe longe to live: strength her that she may vanquish and overcome al her enemies: And finally after this life she may attaine everlasting joye and felicitie, thorowe Jesus Christ our Lorde.
Amen.
