# [A Prayer that may be said after any of the former]

<!-- VERIFY: 'untitled' — the source prints this prayer with no title; the bracketed heading is editorial -->

O GOD, whose nature and propertie is ever to have mercy, and to forgive, receive our humble petitions: and though we be tied and bound with the chaine of our sins: yet let the pitifulnesse of thy great mercy loose us, for Jesus Christ his sake our Mediator and Advocate.
Amen.
